## What You Will Learn
Title: Learn Beyond Memorising Medicines
Intro: Pharmacy practice requires more than remembering medicine names. During this course, students develop an understanding of:
- Why medicines are used
- How medicines work
- How prescriptions are interpreted
- How medicines are safely handled
- How dispensing procedures are followed
- How medicine doses and quantities are calculated
- How pharmacy stock is managed
- How pharmaceutical knowledge is applied in real-life situations

Closing: Our goal is to help students move from simply knowing information to understanding how to use it.

## Course Modules
1. Foundations of Pharmacy Practice — Understand the role of pharmacy personnel, professional responsibilities and the basic structure of pharmacy practice.
2. Medical Terminology — Learn common medical and pharmaceutical terms used in prescriptions, medicine labels and healthcare communication.
3. Dosage Forms & Drug Administration — Understand tablets, capsules, liquids, creams, inhalers, injections and other dosage forms, including common routes of administration.
4. Pharmaceutical Packaging & Quality Assurance — Learn the importance of medicine packaging, labelling, storage and maintaining product quality.
5. Prescription Handling & Safe Medication Practices — Develop an understanding of prescription terminology, medicine instructions, dispensing safety and common medication errors.
6. Pharmaceutical Calculations — Learn calculations used in pharmacy practice, including doses, quantities and medicine measurements.
7. Assisting in Dispensing Medications — Understand the basic dispensing process, prescription interpretation, labelling and medicine handling.
8. Therapeutics of Common Diseases — Learn commonly used medicines for major health conditions and understand their basic uses, actions and important safety considerations.
9. Pharmacy Law & Regulations — Gain an introduction to the legal and professional requirements relevant to pharmacy practice.
10. Practical Pharmacy Skills — Apply your knowledge through activities, pharmacy scenarios, educational games and practical learning exercises.
